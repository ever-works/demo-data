# Privacy Policy

This is a demonstration site for the Ever Works platform.

- If you create a demo account, we store the name, email and password hash you
  supply so the sign-in flow can be demonstrated.
- Demo accounts and any content you submit may be deleted at any time without notice.
- Please do **not** enter real personal data, real payment details, or anything
  confidential into this demo.
- Payments run in Stripe **test** mode only.

For the privacy policy governing the Ever Works product, see
[ever.works](https://ever.works).
